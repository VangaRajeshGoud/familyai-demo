The problem

Same list, every week

Families rebuild the same grocery list again and again: monthly staples, the Sunday chicken, fruit, vegetables and water cans.

Budget in your head

Most households track the monthly grocery budget mentally and only notice overspending at the end of the month.

Health gets missed

It's hard to spot ingredients that clash with a family member's dietary needs while shopping in a hurry.

How FamilyAI works

1

Tell it once

Family size, food preferences, organic choices, budget and routines, set up once in the family profile.

2

Just speak

"Add my usual Sunday basket." FamilyAI builds the basket with every ingredient, within budget.

3

Review & approve

Health nudges and savings are shown first. Nothing is ordered without the family's approval.

Business model

🤝 Partner commission

A share of every basket routed to grocery, meat, produce and water partners.

🏷️ Sponsored defaults

Brands become the family's default supplier for a category, such as "Sunday chicken by [Partner]".

⭐ Family Premium

A monthly subscription for health tracking, multi-member profiles and nutrition coaching.
